About Africannese Steelworks & Construction

Africannese Steelworks & Construction provides structural steel fabrication, erection and reinforcement support services for infrastructure projects, contractor developments and institutional facilities across South Africa and the African continent. With over 15 years of hands-on fabrication and installation experience, the division supports municipalities, construction companies, schools, churches and private developers requiring dependable structural steel solutions delivered safely and professionally.
