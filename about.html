Who We Are

Africannese Group is a South African multi-division company delivering services in structural steelworks, construction support services, corporate wear supply, PPE distribution, and branding solutions. Our operations are structured into two specialised divisions: • Africannese Steelworks & Construction • Africannese Corporate Wear & Branding

Industry Experience

With over 15 years of experience in steel fabrication, welding, and steel erection, Africannese provides dependable workmanship aligned with construction industry requirements across South Africa. We support contractors, municipalities, developers, institutions, and private sector clients.

Our Services

Steelworks Division: • Structural steel fabrication • General welding • Steel erection • Industrial installations

Corporate Wear Division: • Corporate uniforms • PPE supply • Embroidery services • Printing & branding solutions

Our Commitment

Africannese Group is committed to delivering reliable service, professional workmanship, and quality solutions aligned with client expectations across both construction and corporate supply environments.
