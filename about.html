Who We Are

Africannese Group is a multi-division engineering and workforce support company delivering structural steel fabrication, construction support services and professional corporate uniform supply solutions across South Africa and the African continent. The organisation operates through two specialised divisions designed to support infrastructure development projects, contractor operations, institutions and workforce identity programmes with reliable execution capacity and dependable supply capability.

Our Divisions

Africannese operates through the following specialised business units:

  • Africannese Steelworks & Construction — structural steel fabrication, erection, container conversions, carports, shelters and infrastructure reinforcement support
  • Africannese Corporate Wear — bulk workforce uniforms, reflective safety wear, golf shirts, branded apparel and identity solutions

Our Service Footprint

Africannese supports projects and supply programmes across:

  • South Africa
  • Southern Africa Region
  • Infrastructure support environments across the African continent
