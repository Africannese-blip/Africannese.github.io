Who We Are

Africannese Group is a South African multi-division company providing professional services in structural steelworks, construction support services, corporate wear supply, PPE distribution, and branding solutions. Our operations are structured into two specialised divisions: • Africannese Steelworks & Construction • Africannese Corporate Wear & Branding This structure allows us to deliver focused expertise across engineering and supply environments while maintaining a unified service standard.

Our Experience

With over 15 years of industry experience in steel fabrication, welding, and steel erection, Africannese delivers reliable workmanship aligned with construction industry requirements. We support contractors, municipalities, developers, institutions, and private sector clients with both project-based services and supply solutions.

Our Services

Our Steelworks Division provides: • Structural steel fabrication • General welding services • Steel erection and installations • Industrial support projects Our Corporate Wear Division provides: • Corporate uniforms • PPE supply • Embroidery services • Printing and branding solutions

Our Commitment

Africannese Group is committed to delivering dependable service, quality workmanship, and professional solutions aligned with client requirements across both construction and corporate supply sectors.
