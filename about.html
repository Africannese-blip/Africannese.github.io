Company Profile

Africannese Group is a 100% black-owned South African business providing steel fabrication, construction, corporate wear, PPE supply and branding solutions. We serve homeowners, churches, contractors, businesses and public sector clients with a commitment to quality, safety and professionalism.

Vision, Mission & Values

Vision

To become a trusted African engineering and construction brand recognised for quality and innovation.

Mission

To deliver dependable engineering, construction and corporate supply solutions while creating sustainable employment.

Core Values

Integrity, Excellence, Safety, Innovation, Accountability and Customer Satisfaction.
