About Africannese Steelworks & Construction

Africannese Steelworks & Construction has over 15 years of experience in steel fabrication, welding repairs, steel erecting, container converting and painting. We are committed to delivering safe, durable, and high-quality steel structures across Pretoria and Gauteng. Our vision is to transform strong ideas into strong structures while ensuring customer satisfaction at every project.
