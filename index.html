About Us

Africannese Steelworks & Construction is a South African–based steel fabrication and construction company specialising in the fabrication, installation, and maintenance of structural steel solutions.

The company provides professional services to the commercial, industrial, institutional, and private sectors. Our scope of work includes steel fabrication, steel erecting, container conversions, protective steel painting, and general steel-related construction works.

Africannese Steelworks & Construction is committed to quality workmanship, safety compliance, and cost-effective service delivery. Projects are executed with proper planning, adherence to specifications, and alignment with applicable safety standards.

The company is positioned to support small to medium-scale projects and can scale operations according to project requirements. Our objective is to deliver structurally sound solutions that offer long-term durability and value.

Our Services

Steel Fabrication

Fabrication of structural steel components including beams, columns, frames, carports, and custom steel works in accordance with drawings and specifications.

Steel Erecting

On-site erection and installation of structural steel structures, ensuring correct alignment, stability, and compliance with safety standards.

Container Conversions

Conversion of shipping containers into workshops, offices, storage units, and functional working spaces using steel modifications and finishing works.

Painting & Protective Coatings

Industrial and protective steel painting to enhance durability, corrosion resistance, and professional appearance.

Construction Support Works

General steel-related construction works including structural reinforcements and supporting installations.
