About Us

Africannese Steelworks & Construction is a trusted name in steel fabrication, welding repairs, container converting, painting, steel erecting, and general construction. With over 15 years of hands-on experience, we deliver durable, safe, and cost-effective solutions for industrial, commercial, and private clients.

Our mission is simple: turn strong ideas into strong, long-lasting structures through quality workmanship, professionalism, and attention to detail.

Our Services

  • Steel Fabrication: Custom steel structures designed and fabricated to meet industrial and commercial standards.
  • Welding Repairs: Reliable on-site and workshop welding repairs for machinery, structures, and equipment.
  • Container Converting: Conversion of shipping containers into offices, storage units, workshops, and accommodation.
  • Painting: Industrial and commercial painting for corrosion protection and professional finishes.
  • Steel Erecting: Safe and precise erection of steel structures, warehouses, and frameworks.
  • Construction: General construction services including structural installation and project support.
