About Us

Africannese Steelworks & Construction is a professionally managed steel fabrication and construction company delivering structural steel solutions for commercial, industrial, institutional and private sector projects.

Our core expertise includes steel fabrication, steel erecting, container conversions, protective steel painting, carports, and general construction works. We focus on producing structurally sound, durable, and cost-effective solutions that comply with industry standards and safety requirements.

We are committed to quality workmanship, project efficiency, and safety compliance. Every project is executed with attention to detail, proper planning, and close coordination with clients to ensure timelines and specifications are met.

Africannese Steelworks & Construction is positioned to support both small-scale and large-scale projects, offering reliable service delivery and long-term structural performance. Our goal is to build lasting partnerships through trust, consistency, and excellence.

Project Experience

Our completed projects include steel structural support installations, container workshop conversions, multiple carport developments, and steel reinforcement works for educational and private facilities.

Each project is executed with strict attention to safety, structural integrity, and client requirements, ensuring reliable and long-lasting results.
