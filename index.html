About Us

Africannese Steelworks & Construction is a professional steel and construction company delivering reliable, durable, and high-quality steel solutions. With over 15 years of hands-on industry experience, we have built a strong reputation for precision, safety, and workmanship excellence.

We serve industrial, commercial, and private clients, offering tailor-made steel solutions that meet project specifications, deadlines, and budget requirements.

Our Services

  • Steel Fabrication: High-quality workshop fabrication of beams, frames, platforms, gates, and custom steel components.
  • Steel Erecting: Professional on-site erection of steel structures including warehouses, workshops, and roofing systems.
  • Welding Repairs: Structural and industrial welding repairs ensuring strength, safety, and durability.
  • Container Converting: Conversion of containers into offices, storage units, guard rooms, and custom solutions.
  • Painting & Construction: Protective steel painting, corrosion control, and general construction services.

Past Projects

Africannese Steelworks & Construction has successfully completed a wide range of projects including industrial steel structures, container conversions, residential steel works, workshop fabrication projects, and on-site structural repairs.

Our projects are executed with strict safety standards, quality control, and a strong commitment to delivering results that exceed client expectations.
