About Us

Africannese Steelworks & Construction is a professional steel and construction company with over 15 years of industry experience. We specialize in steel fabrication, steel erecting, welding repairs, container converting, painting, and general construction.

Our team is committed to delivering durable, safe, and high-quality structures for industrial, commercial, and private clients across Southern Africa.

Our Core Services

  • Steel Fabrication: Workshop-based fabrication of strong, precise steel components.
  • Steel Erecting: On-site erection of steel structures, warehouses, and frameworks.
  • Welding Repairs: Structural and industrial welding repairs.
  • Container Converting: Custom container offices, storage units, and structures.
  • Painting & Construction: Protective coatings and general construction services.
