About Us

Africannese Steelworks & Construction is a professional steel fabrication and construction company specialising in steel erecting, container conversions, carports and structural steel solutions. We deliver strong, safe and reliable structures with precision and pride.

Steel Erecting

Professional onsite steel erecting for buildings, warehouses, schools and industrial structures using safe and approved methods.

Container Conversions

Conversion of shipping containers into offices, workshops, storage units and accommodation spaces.

Carports & Shade Structures

Strong and durable carports for residential, commercial and fleet use.
