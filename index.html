About Us

Africannese Steelworks & Construction has over 15 years of experience delivering high-quality steel fabrication, welding repairs, container converting, painting, steel erecting, and general construction services across Southern Africa. We specialize in turning strong ideas into strong structures, providing durable solutions tailored to each client’s needs. Our team is professional, safety-conscious, and committed to excellence on every project.

Our Services

  • Steel Fabrication: We design and fabricate steel structures for industrial and commercial projects, ensuring durability and precision.
  • Welding Repairs: Professional welding repairs for machinery, structures, and equipment, using high-quality materials and techniques.
  • Container Converting: Transform shipping containers into offices, homes, storage units, or specialized industrial spaces.
  • Painting: Industrial and commercial painting services with long-lasting finishes and corrosion protection.
  • Construction: General construction services including structural installation, steel erection, and site management.
  • Steel Erecting: Expert steel erection services for warehouses, factories, and large structures, ensuring safety, precision, and timely completion.
