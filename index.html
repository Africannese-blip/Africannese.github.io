Company Profile

Africannese Steelworks & Construction is a leading steel and construction company with over 15 years of experience delivering durable and high-quality steel solutions.

We specialize in steel fabrication, steel erecting, welding repairs, container converting, painting, and general construction. Our skilled team ensures projects are completed efficiently, safely, and to the highest standards.

Our Core Services

  • Steel Fabrication: Precision workshop fabrication of steel components.
  • Steel Erecting: Safe and accurate on-site erection of steel structures.
  • Welding Repairs: Structural and industrial welding repairs.
  • Container Converting: Offices, storage units, and custom container solutions.
  • Painting & Construction: Protective coatings and general construction work.
