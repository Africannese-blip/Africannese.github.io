About Africannese

Africannese Steelworks & Construction is a developing South African contractor specialising in structural steel fabrication, steel erection, infrastructure support works and professional corporate wear supply. The company supports public sector projects, private developments, schools, commercial property upgrades and subcontracting partnerships through reliable workmanship and practical site experience.
