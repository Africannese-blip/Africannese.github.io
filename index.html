About Africannese Steelworks & Construction

Africannese Steelworks & Construction is a Pretoria-based structural steel fabrication and erection company delivering reliable workmanship across residential, commercial, education and infrastructure support projects. With over 15 years of industry experience in steel fabrication, welding and structural erection, the company provides safe, compliant and cost-effective solutions aligned with construction industry standards and client specifications. Our services support contractors, schools, churches, developers and private clients requiring dependable structural installations, container conversions, carports, shelters and reinforcement works. Africannese Steelworks & Construction is committed to quality delivery, professional site conduct and meeting project timelines within budget expectations.
