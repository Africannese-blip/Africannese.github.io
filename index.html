About Us

Africannese Steelworks & Construction is a professional steel and construction company with over 15 years of experience delivering reliable, durable, and high-quality steel solutions.

We specialize in steel fabrication, steel erecting, welding repairs, container converting, painting, and general construction for industrial, commercial, and private clients.

Our Core Services

  • Steel Fabrication: Precision workshop fabrication of steel components.
  • Steel Erecting: Safe and accurate on-site erection of steel structures.
  • Welding Repairs: Structural and industrial welding repairs.
  • Container Converting: Offices, storage units, and custom container solutions.
  • Painting & Construction: Protective coatings and general construction work.
