About Africannese Steelworks & Construction

Africannese Steelworks & Construction provides structural steel fabrication and erection services across South Africa and the African continent. With over 15 years industry experience in fabrication, welding and structural installation, the company supports contractors, schools, municipalities, churches, developers and infrastructure projects requiring dependable steel solutions delivered safely and professionally.

Steelworks Services

  • Structural Steel Fabrication
  • Steel Erection
  • Carports & Shelters
  • Container Conversions
  • I-Beam Structural Support Installations
  • Roof Reinforcement Structures
  • On-site Welding Services

Project Experience

  • Stanza Bopape Secondary School — Structural I-beam installation
  • Pravish Engineering Workspace — Conversion of 8 workshop containers
  • David Private Development — Installation of 12 vehicle carports

Company Capability

Africannese Steelworks & Construction supports subcontracting and direct project execution within residential, commercial, education and infrastructure support environments across South Africa and the African continent. The company delivers reliable fabrication capacity, installation expertise and site-ready workforce support aligned with construction project requirements.
